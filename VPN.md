### VPN Privacy Tools – By Umbra

\- Mullvad VPN — [https://mullvad.net/] 
— Privacy-focused VPN with anonymous account options. 
— Hacked? No publicly confirmed major breach found in the sources checked; security vulnerabilities have been disclosed.

\- Proton VPN — [https://protonvpn.com/] 
— Audited no-logs VPN with open-source clients. 
— Hacked? No publicly confirmed major VPN infrastructure breach found in the sources checked; this is not proof that no incident has ever occurred.

\- IVPN — [https://www.ivpn.net/] 
— Privacy-focused VPN with published security audits. 
— Hacked? A security incident affected its Bitcoin payment server in August 2026; this was a payment-infrastructure incident, not evidence that VPN traffic was exposed.

\- Windscribe — [https://windscribe.com/] 
— VPN with privacy features and a published no-logs policy. 
— Hacked? The company reported a server seizure in the Netherlands in February 2026; it said no user logs were found.

\- NordVPN — [https://nordvpn.com/] 
— Commercial VPN with independently audited no-logs claims. 
— Hacked? Yes. One rented server in Finland was accessed without authorization in 2018; the incident was publicly disclosed in 2019.
